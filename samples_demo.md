# Sample responses (demo mode)

### How do I verify as a student?

Students verify with a school email address or by uploading a current enrollment document. Graduates can no longer use student discounts.

- Retrieved: student-docs, verify-how, military

### My verification failed, what now?

If verification fails, check that your name matches your document exactly and that the document is not expired. You can retry up to three times, then contact support for manual review.

- Retrieved: failed, verify-how, expiry

### Can I use two coupon codes on one order?

### Is it safe? Will shops see my personal info?

Stores only learn that you belong to an eligible group. They do not receive your documents or personal records. You can delete your account and stored data at any time.

### Can my spouse use my military discount?

Active duty, reservists, veterans, and military spouses can verify using their service records. Family members may qualify at stores that include military families.
